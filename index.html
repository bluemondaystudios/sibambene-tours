What we offer

One call. Everyone moves together.

Tours

Day trips and weekend getaways along the Panorama Route, Kruger gates, Sudwala and further afield. We drive, you take the photos.

Ceremonies

Weddings, umembeso, funerals, graduations and traditional ceremonies. We bring the family in, together and dressed for the day.

Mass gatherings

Church conferences, sports days, rallies and stokvel outings. Multiple trips and pickup points can be co-ordinated for big groups.

Accommodation

Need somewhere to stay? We help arrange lodges and guesthouses along your route so the trip is sorted end to end.

Meet the leader & hands-on driver

Dennis

Sibambene Tours is not run from behind a desk. The person who answers your call is very often the person behind the wheel on the day.

Dennis started Sibambene with a simple idea: a group that travels together arrives together. Every booking gets a personal touch, from planning the route and the stops to making sure the music is right and the aircon is on before the first passenger climbs in.

“When you book with us, you’re not a number. You’re family on the road, and we hold that responsibility together.”
